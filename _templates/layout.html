How to Access and Login to Your Old Bellsouth.net Email Account? Steps for the Existing Users

If you have an old Bellsouth.net email account, you may be wondering whether you can still access your messages and how to complete the Bellsouth.net email login process. The good news is that eligible legacy BellSouth email addresses can still be accessed through AT&T Mail.

BellSouth became part of AT&T after AT&T acquired BellSouth in 2006. Today, eligible Bellsouth.net email accounts are supported through AT&T Mail, while you can continue using your existing Bellsouth.net email address.

This guide explains how to access your old Bellsouth email account, sign in, reset a forgotten password, and troubleshoot common login problems.

Can You Still Access an Old Bellsouth.net Email Account?

Yes. If your Bellsouth.net mailbox is eligible and remains active, you can continue accessing it through AT&T Mail. Your email address does not necessarily need to change simply because BellSouth is now part of AT&T.

AT&T supports eligible legacy email addresses ending in @bellsouth.net. This means an existing Bellsouth.net address can be used to sign in to AT&T Mail.

How to Perform the Bellsouth.net Email Login

The easiest way to access your old BellSouth mailbox is through AT&T's current mail service.

  1. Open the official AT&T Mail sign-in page.
  2. Choose the option to sign in to your mail account.
  3. Enter your complete Bellsouth.net email address.
  4. Enter the password associated with your account.
  5. Complete any security verification requested during sign-in.
  6. After successful authentication, you should be taken to your mailbox.

What Happened to Bellsouth.net Email?

BellSouth was a telecommunications company that operated primarily in the southeastern United States. AT&T acquired BellSouth in 2006, and BellSouth services became part of AT&T.

Although the BellSouth brand is no longer used as it once was, eligible legacy email addresses ending in @bellsouth.net can continue to be used through AT&T Mail.

Therefore, if you are searching for the old Bellsouth email login page, you generally do not need to look for a separate BellSouth email service. Your legacy mailbox is accessed through AT&T's mail system.

How to Recover Your Bellsouth Email Password

If you remember your Bellsouth.net email address but have forgotten the password, you can use AT&T's password recovery process.

  1. Open the AT&T Mail sign-in page.
  2. Choose the password recovery or reset option.
  3. Enter the requested account information.
  4. Complete the identity verification steps.
  5. Create a new password when prompted.
  6. Return to the AT&T Mail sign-in page and try logging in again.

What If Your Bellsouth.net Email Login Is Not Working?

There are several reasons an old Bellsouth email account may fail to sign in. Before assuming that the account has been lost, try these basic troubleshooting steps.

1. Check Your Email Address

Make sure you are entering the complete email address, including the @bellsouth.net portion. An incorrect domain or spelling mistake can prevent authentication.

2. Check Your Password

Passwords are case-sensitive. Check that Caps Lock is not enabled and that you are using the current password rather than an older password saved in your browser.

3. Reset the Password

If you cannot remember the correct password, use AT&T's account recovery options instead of repeatedly entering incorrect passwords.

4. Try a Different Browser

Browser cookies, cached data, extensions, or outdated browser software can sometimes interfere with sign-in. Try a private or incognito browser window or use another supported browser.

5. Check Whether the Mailbox Is Inactive

AT&T states that an AT&T mailbox that has not been used for 12 months may be considered inactive. An inactive mailbox may be recoverable for up to three years by signing in, although the original mailbox contents and settings may not be restored.

How to Access Bellsouth.net Email on a Phone or Email App

You can also configure an eligible Bellsouth.net account in a supported email application. Depending on the application and authentication method, you may need a secure mail key.

AT&T's published settings for supported legacy email domains, including Bellsouth.net, include the following:

Protocol Incoming Server Port Outgoing Server Port
IMAP imap.mail.att.net 993 smtp.mail.att.net 465 or 587
POP3 inbound.att.net 995 outbound.att.net 465

Do You Need a Secure Mail Key?

Some third-party email applications may require a secure mail key if they do not support OAuth authentication. AT&T recommends creating a secure mail key for supported apps that require it.

Applications that support OAuth may not require a secure mail key. The exact requirements depend on the email application and device you are using.

Tips for Keeping Your Old Bellsouth Email Account Accessible

  • Sign in periodically so the mailbox does not become inactive.
  • Keep your recovery information up to date.
  • Use a strong, unique password.
  • Never share your password or verification codes with anyone.
  • Be cautious of emails claiming that your Bellsouth.net account will be deleted unless you provide your login details.
  • Use official AT&T websites when resetting your password or managing your account.

Frequently Asked Questions About Bellsouth.net Email Login

Is Bellsouth.net email still available?

Eligible legacy Bellsouth.net email accounts can still be accessed through AT&T Mail. Availability depends on the status and eligibility of the individual account.

Where do I log in to my old Bellsouth email account?

Use the current AT&T Mail sign-in service and enter your existing Bellsouth.net email address and password.

Did my Bellsouth.net email address change to AT&T?

Not necessarily. Eligible legacy users can continue using their existing @bellsouth.net address through AT&T Mail.

What should I do if I forgot my Bellsouth email password?

Use AT&T's password recovery process to verify your account and create a new password. If recovery is unsuccessful, contact AT&T support for further assistance.

Can I use Bellsouth.net email with Outlook or another email app?

Yes. AT&T provides POP3 and IMAP settings for supported email applications, although the exact setup and authentication requirements can vary by app.

Final Thoughts

Accessing an old Bellsouth.net email account is still possible for eligible active accounts. Instead of searching for an outdated BellSouth login page, use AT&T Mail and sign in with your existing Bellsouth.net email address and password.

If you cannot sign in, first verify your email address and password, then use AT&T's password recovery and account support options. For users who prefer an email application, AT&T also provides current IMAP and POP3 settings for Bellsouth.net accounts.

Using the official AT&T Mail service is the safest approach for completing your Bellsouth.net email login and managing your legacy mailbox.
